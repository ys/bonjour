I am Yannick Schutz, a **documentary and lifestyle photographer** based in the Monts d'Arrée, in Finistère, Brittany. I photograph real people doing real things, mostly on film. For brands, that means pictures that look like life, not like an ad.

I work in Brittany and wherever the job is. [Version française](/travail/).

## What I photograph

**Brand campaigns and lookbooks.** Lifestyle images for brands that want honest pictures of people using their things: outdoors, by the sea, at home. Recently: a collab with [TWOTHIRDS](https://twothirds.com).

**Editorial stories.** Documentary stories for magazines and publications, from a single portrait to a full reportage.

**Surf.** I surf myself, so I am at home photographing surfers, shapers and the culture around them, from the beach, the parking lot and the shaping bay, and in the water with a [Nikonos V](/nikonos-v/) when the surf is small.

**Prints and zines.** I make zines and signed prints. My [first zine](/shop/a-thousand-tides/) sold out with Ex Why Zed, and you can find the current ones in the [shop](/shop/). Get in touch for custom prints or editions.

## How I work

I shoot mostly on film (Leica M6, Contax T3, Nikonos V), with digital Leicas (M10-P, SL2-S) when a project needs it. I prefer small crews, natural light and real moments over staged setups.
