  Fin de service with @macdemarco - Shot at Le Bar Fleuri , Paris - July 2023


  Seated in a small Parisian bistro, Mac Demarco and the members of his band
  have just put down their forks. It's the last call, and the kitchen is about
  to close. The band picks up their instruments and starts an impromptu acoustic
  concert for the few regulars of this typical brasserie. Alternating new tunes,
  fan favorites and silly jokes with the mellow energy of a hungover day.
